# My Personal Website

This repository contains the source code and content for my personal website, showcasing my design work, professional experience, and creative projects.

## About

My personal website serves as a central hub for my online presence, featuring:

- **Portfolio & Design Work** – Examples of my creative projects and design aesthetic
- **Personal Branding** – A cohesive representation of my professional identity and history
- **Resume** – My professional experience and qualifications
- **Substack Links** – Connections to my writing and other creative content I produce

The site emphasizes visual design and user experience, reflecting my approach to creative work, digital presence and technology.

## Purpose

This repository exists to:
- Provide a professional online presence
- Share my creative work with potential collaborators and employers
- Demonstrate my skills in web design and development
- Communicate my passion for user experience and love of design as a practice
- Serve as a living portfolio that evolves with my career

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Motion & Animation:** Framer Motion, GSAP (GreenSock Animation Platform)
- **Deployment:** Vercel
- **Hosting:** Vercel
